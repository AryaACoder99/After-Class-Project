Honeybees are amazing social creatures that power our ecosystem and agriculture.

Inside the Hive

The Queen

The heart of the colony, laying thousands of eggs daily to sustain the population.

The Workers

All female, managing everything from foraging to cleaning and defense.

The Drones

Male bees whose primary role is to mate with a new queen.

How Bees Make Magic

Necter & Pollen

Foragers collect nectar for energy and honey, and protein-rich pollen to feed the young larvae.

Waxes

Young worker bees secrete wax from special abdominal glands to build the iconic hexagonal honeycomb.
